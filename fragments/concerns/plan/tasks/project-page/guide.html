How to use this page
The tabs
The project's nine phases, in the order they run. Pick one to read it; the page opens on the one in progress. Under each name is its state: Done, Now, To come or Not needed. A phase in progress also counts its items done, such as 3/6.
Calendar
Who holds the ball: what waits on you, and what to do about it; what the agent does next; and what waits on a date. Below them, the notes for the next session, the months, and every date, newest first.
Items
Each card has a number that never changes, such as D1 or EX-2. Its pill says what merging it releases (Major, Minor or Patch), or that it is yours to do (You) or done together (Together). Amended marks an item added after the plan was approved.
Statuses
Each reads in its item's words: a decision is Taken, a PR Merged. One such as To merge or To decide waits on you; Ready is next for the agent; a blocked one says what it waits on; a dropped one is struck through, with why.
Links
A number with its name, such as D1 Where the probes live, opens that item, whichever phase holds it. Needs lists what comes first; Needed by and Named by, the items that depend on it or mention it.
The board
The PRs and settings in waves, left to right: a wave starts once what it needs is done. Each chip names its status; a done one fades and a blocked one is dashed.
What changed
Each phase ends with its changelog: what changed, and when. The header says when the state was last read.
Answering
The page only shows the state. Answer in your chat with the agent, naming items by number, such as "D3: b".
